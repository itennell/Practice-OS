## Before you start

- Counselors and MFTs: you need a master's or doctorate that qualifies for your license, at least 2 years or 3,000 hours of supervised post-master's clinical experience, and a current state license.
- Have your NPI, license, the practice address, and bank details for direct deposit ready.
- Medicare pays counselors and MFTs 75% of the clinical psychologist rate.

## Steps

1. [You] Sign in with your CMS I&A login (the same one as NPPES).
2. [You] Start a new enrollment in PECOS as an individual practitioner (the online CMS-855I), choosing your specialty: mental health counselor, marriage and family therapist, clinical social worker or clinical psychologist.
3. Add your practice location, license and NPI. Set up direct deposit (CMS-588) in the same application.
4. If your PLLC will bill Medicare, the PLLC enrolls too (CMS-855B, with its Type 2 NPI) and you reassign your payments to it. PECOS walks you through this.
5. Decide whether to be a participating provider (CMS-460), which means you always accept Medicare's rate.
6. [You] Upload documents, then e-sign and submit. Your Medicare contractor (MAC) may email questions; answer them quickly.
7. Once approved, tell Aeon your enrollment date. Revalidation comes every 5 years; Aeon watches data.cms.gov, where CMS posts the due date 7 months ahead.

## Good to know

- Report a new practice location, ownership change or legal action within 30 days, and other changes within 90.
- Through Dec 31 2027, Medicare clients can see you by telehealth from home; after that, in-person visits are required every 12 months.
- Choosing not to take Medicare at all is a separate step (an opt-out affidavit), and it renews itself every 2 years.
