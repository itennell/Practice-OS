## Before you start

- Pick a name that ends in "Professional Limited Liability Company" or "PLLC", and check it's free on the Secretary of State's name search.
- Every member and manager must hold the same license you do (counseling, social work, MFT and clinical psychology are on IDFPR's list).
- Choose a registered agent: you or another Illinois resident, at an Illinois street address (no P.O. box).

## Steps

1. [You] File Articles of Organization (Form LLC-5.5, $150) with the Illinois Secretary of State. Include a purpose clause naming your profession, for example "to provide clinical professional counseling services". The online filing doesn't take articles that need a specific purpose, so use the paper form.
2. Get an EIN for the PLLC from the IRS (free, online at irs.gov). Aeon can fill in the details; you submit it.
3. [You] Apply for the PLLC's certificate of registration on IDFPR's online portal ($50). Use the exact name on your articles, upload a PDF of the filed articles, and enter the EIN and formation date.
4. Repeat step 3 for each office location; each needs its own registration.
5. Open a business bank account in the PLLC's name, and get a Type 2 NPI for it (see the NPI guide).
6. [You] Every year: file the LLC annual report ($75) in the 60 days before the first day of your anniversary month. Late means a $100 penalty and, eventually, dissolution. Aeon puts it on your Deadlines page.
7. [You] Every third year: renew the IDFPR registration ($40). All PLLC registrations expire Jan 1 (2028, 2031 and so on).

## Good to know

- Already formed as a plain LLC? IDFPR asks you to amend to a PLLC (Form LLC-5.25) and add the professional purpose.
- Aeon prepares forms and reminders; it doesn't sign or submit for you, and this isn't legal advice. An attorney can review your articles.
