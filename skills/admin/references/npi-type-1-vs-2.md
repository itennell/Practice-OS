## Which one do you need

- Type 1 is you, the clinician. You only ever get one, and it follows you between jobs and states.
- Type 2 is an organization, such as your PLLC or a group practice. A clinician who has incorporated can have both: a Type 1 for herself and a Type 2 for the PLLC.
- A sole proprietor with no PLLC uses only her Type 1.

## Steps

1. [You] Create a login in CMS's Identity & Access system (I&A). You'll use the same login for NPPES and, later, Medicare's PECOS.
2. [You] Apply for your Type 1 NPI online in NPPES. It's free and the online way is fastest.
3. Pick your taxonomy code by license: counselor (LCPC or LPC) 101YM0800X, clinical social worker 1041C0700X, marriage and family therapist 106H00000X, clinical psychologist 103TC0700X.
4. [You] If you have a PLLC, apply for its Type 2 NPI the same way, using the PLLC's legal name and EIN, with you as the authorized official.
5. Save both numbers; Aeon adds them to your credentialing profile for CAQH and payers.
6. [You] Whenever your address, name, phone or taxonomy changes, update NPPES within 30 days. Aeon starts a 30-day clock when you log the change.

## Good to know

- An NPI doesn't license, credential or enroll you anywhere; payers and Medicare are separate steps.
- Your NPI record is public in the NPI Registry, so use your practice address, not your home.
