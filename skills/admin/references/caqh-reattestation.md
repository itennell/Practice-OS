## Before you start

- Have your current license, malpractice certificate and any recently changed documents as PDFs.
- If your license or malpractice policy has expired in CAQH, it will make you update it before you can attest.

## Steps

1. [You] Sign in to CAQH ProView.
2. If anything changed (address, hours, insurance, malpractice), open Profile Data and update that section.
3. Upload any new or renewed documents under Documents.
4. Click Review and Attest. Fix anything listed under View errors.
5. [You] Answer the Illinois disclosure questions if CAQH asks for them.
6. [You] Click Attest. You'll see Attestation Completed, and an email confirmation follows within about 48 hours.
7. Tell Aeon the date you attested, so the next reminder lands 180 days later.

## Good to know

- Miss the date and your profile shows Expired the next day; payers may stop reading it until you re-attest.
- CAQH emails you before it's due, then again 14, 28 and 42 days after it expires.
- Only you can attest. Aeon can check your documents and list what changed, but the attestation is yours.
