## Before you start

- Get your NPI and pick your taxonomy code (see the NPI guide).
- Make sure your IDFPR license is current; IMPACT checks it at enrollment and at every revalidation.
- [You] Send HFS a certified W-9 if you'll be paid directly (subject line "W9 Certification").
- Use Microsoft Edge or Firefox. Safari doesn't work with IMPACT and some fields won't fill in.

## Steps

1. [You] Create your IMPACT login (an ILogin with your email address).
2. [You] Start a new enrollment as an individual practitioner (or a sole proprietor or group if your PLLC bills).
3. Enter your NPI, taxonomy, license, practice location and payment details. Aeon can prefill these from your profile.
4. [You] Review, sign and submit. HFS emails you, so keep that address current.
5. Read the HFS Medical Provider Handbook for your provider type before you see Medicaid clients.
6. Once approved, find your revalidation date in IMPACT under Basic Information and tell Aeon. HFS emails 90 and 30 days before it.
7. [You] Report any change to your enrollment to HFS right away by updating IMPACT.

## Good to know

- Revalidation is at least every 5 years. Miss it and you're disenrolled, and coverage isn't restored retroactively.
- Illinois Medicaid claims must be filed within 180 days of the date of service.
- Don't start a revalidation until HFS emails you that your cycle is open.
