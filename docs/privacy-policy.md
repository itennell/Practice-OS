# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the text messages sent by **Caseload** (a sole proprietorship), the developer of Practice-OS, an open-source front-desk assistant for small counseling practices. It explains what we collect when you call or text a practice line that uses Practice-OS, and how that information is used.

During the current demo phase, texts are sent only to the developer's own verified phone. The demo practice, Reyes Counseling, is fictional.

## What we collect

When you call the practice line and leave a voicemail, or text the practice line, we collect:

- your phone number
- the name you give in your voicemail or text
- your voicemail recording and a written transcript of it
- the text messages you send and receive
- the consultation time you choose, if you book one

We do not ask for, and you should not send, health or treatment information by text.

## How we use it

We use this information only to:

- text you up to three open times for a free 15-minute consultation
- book, reschedule or cancel that consultation when you reply
- send one reminder the day before a booked consultation
- pass questions we can't answer to the clinician
- send an urgent alert to the clinician if a message mentions a crisis or danger

We do not use your information for marketing or advertising.

## Who processes it

To deliver this service, your information is handled by:

- **Twilio**, the phone and text carrier, which delivers calls and texts
- **Meta**, whose AI model transcribes voicemails and drafts scheduling replies
- the practice's own scheduling calendar (Cal.com or Google Calendar), which receives only a booking reference such as "Phone consult FD-1042", never your name

Records are stored in the practice's own Practice-OS database.

**We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply **HELP** for help. See our [Terms & Conditions](terms.md) for the full SMS terms.

## Keeping and deleting your information

Voicemail transcripts and text history are kept only as long as the practice needs them to schedule your consultation. You can ask for your information to be deleted by contacting us.
