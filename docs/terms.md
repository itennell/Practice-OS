# Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 28, 2026

These Terms & Conditions cover the scheduling text messages sent by **Caseload** (a sole proprietorship), the developer of Practice-OS, an open-source front-desk assistant for small counseling practices.

During the current demo phase, texts are sent only to the developer's own verified phone. The demo practice, Reyes Counseling, is fictional.

## SMS Terms

**Program name:** Caseload practice scheduling texts.

**What you'll receive:** after you call the practice line and leave a voicemail, you'll get one text from the practice's AI assistant with up to three open times for a free 15-minute consultation. After that, you only get texts in reply to your own texts (booking, rescheduling or cancelling), plus one reminder the day before a booked consultation. These texts are about scheduling only. They never give health or treatment advice, and they are never marketing.

**How you opt in:** by calling the practice line and leaving a voicemail asking to be contacted. The voicemail greeting tells you that you'll get a text, that message and data rates may apply, and that you can reply STOP to opt out. See [how opt-in works](sms-opt-in.md).

**Message frequency:** varies. Usually 1 to 4 messages per consultation request.

**Message and data rates may apply.**

**To stop:** reply **STOP**, CANCEL, END, QUIT or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no more texts after that. Reply **START** to resubscribe.

**For help:** reply **HELP** or INFO, or call the practice line.

**In an emergency:** these texts are not monitored for emergencies. Call **911**, or call or text **988** for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

**Carriers:** mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Privacy:** see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
