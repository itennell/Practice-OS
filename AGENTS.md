# Practice-OS

## What it is
An open-source AI co-owner, named Aeon, for independently licensed mental health clinicians (LCSW, LCPC, LMFT, psychologist) who run their own private practice in any US state. Illinois is the first state with maintainer-verified rules; other states are researched by Aeon's state-research skill and confirmed by the clinician. It helps them get clients faster and takes the business admin off their plate. It is practice operations software, not clinical care: it never gives medical or mental health advice.

## How the clinician uses it
- She is not technical. She tells any AI coding agent "install Practice-OS from this repo". INSTALL.md walks that agent through every step, asks her for each account in plain words, starts the app and opens http://localhost:3000.
- She works only in the web app, never in the coding agent. The app opens on Aeon's onboarding interview, which sets up the whole practice in 8 chapters: you and your practice; license and rules; fees, insurance and hours; who you help; stage (beginning: just opening; middle: building a caseload; late: established); your tools; how Aeon asks before acting; your team. One question per screen, with Back, Skip and Set up later on each; every answer saves as she goes, and anything she put off shows on the home page as "Still to set up". The stage decides which helpers switch on first.
- After onboarding the app opens on a conversation with Aeon (chat-first home): Aeon's orb, a greeting, the message box and suggestion tiles, with what is waiting for her approval and the morning brief under it. Admin (`/admin`) opens on Aeon's ranked list of what the practice needs next, built in plain code from the morning brief, credentialing gaps, Good Faith Estimates, insurance dates and superbills, with six cards under it (Deadlines, Client forms, Credentialing, Insurance, How do I, Office hours and fees); its forms open in a side panel already filled in. Approvals, Referrals, Files and recent chats are in the chat menu on the left (UI-005: the page colour with a hairline edge in light mode, one step darker than the page in dark mode), which slides in on phones. The same tools are also exposed through an MCP server so any agent can use them.

## The two helpers
1. Admin: compliance calendar (license renewal, CE hours, CAQH re-attestation, Medicare revalidation, PLLC annual report, quarterly taxes, and employer, payroll, HIPAA and local rules), built from rules that each carry a jurisdiction, an official source URL, a quote and a last-checked date. Federal and Illinois rules ship verified; for any other state the state-research skill researches the rules, they reach the clinician as a pending approval, and they stay marked "Aeon researched, please confirm" until she confirms them. Stale rules are re-checked against their source. "How do I" guides, credentialing prep (reading payer PDF forms), Good Faith Estimates.
2. Referrals: find local referral sources with the free NPPES NPI Registry API, track them on a pipeline board, draft outreach letters, follow-up reminders. An approved letter can be copied and sent by the clinician from her own email; Aeon never sends. In the fictional demo, letters go only to Isaiah's own test inbox.

## Design system
- Font Inter 400-700. Calm warm neutrals (like Claude Code), no glows. Light: page #FAF9F5, cards #FFFFFF, ink #1F1E1D, muted text #65635C, hairlines #E3E1D9, primary button #1F1E1D. Dark: page #262624, cards #30302E, ink #F5F4EF, muted text #B5B3AB, primary button #F5F4EF. Purple is only the accent (#5B47C9, hover #43349A; dark #8E7CF8, with #B0A3FB for accent text and links) and Aeon's orb. Success #2F7A55.
- Calm layout (2026-10-28 redesign, Cal.com-inspired, nothing copied): one flat page colour, a quiet menu (16px lucide icons, 14px labels, counts as plain numbers), one display title per screen at most (`--text-display`, Home only), lists as one card with hairline rows.
- Cards: solid (no glass blur on everyday screens), 1px hairline border, radius 20-28px. Frosted glass is only for the opening moments over the living background.
- Aeon is a breathing orb: radial gradient from white to #E3D9FF to #8E7CF8 to #4E69DA, scaling 1 to 1.06 over 4 seconds (no motion if the user prefers reduced motion). Helper orb: Referrals violet (#A77BF0 to #6A3FC8).
- Liquid Aeon (the orb's active state): when Aeon or a helper is large on screen (onboarding, the chat) or actively working, the orb becomes a chrome liquid blob (components/agent-blob) that breathes when resting, splashes when it appears and wobbles while working. Aeon has two plain eyes there, only where the clinician sees it. Small orbs (sidebar, lists) stay the CSS orb. Reduced motion or no WebGL shows the still orb.
- Buttons are pills: primary 50-54px tall, dark #1F1E1D (light #F5F4EF in dark mode); accent 44px #5B47C9; chips 40px (44px when used as tabs or filters). Inputs are 48px pills. Forms use the shared controls only: `Button`, `ButtonLink`, `Chip`, `ChipLink` (components/ui.tsx), `SettingSelect` and `TextField` (components/shared/settings-controls.tsx) and `DateField` (components/shared/date-field.tsx: type a date or pick it from the shared Calendar in a popover). No native `<select>` or date inputs. Aeon's chat bubbles are light with the top-left corner cut; the clinician's are dark with the top-right corner cut.
- Onboarding layout: a 340px glass panel on the left with Aeon's orb, her speech bubble and a step list; the step content on the right. App layout: sidebar left, content right. Touch targets at least 44px. Label all sample data as [Sample].
- UI components: every chat reads docs/ui-elements.md before building UI and uses the shared components listed there instead of inventing its own. shadcn/ui (MIT) on Base UI for primitives; React Bits only for the pieces listed there; never copy coss ui (AGPL) or React Bits Pro code. lucide icons only. No GSAP: animate with motion or CSS. The living Liquid Ether background is for the opening moments only (app opening, onboarding, the demo password page). Colors come from the tokens in app/globals.css, and new ones must pass `npx tsx scripts/contrast.ts` (WCAG AA, light and dark).
- Dictation (the mic in the message box) is for the clinician only.

## Architecture (do not change without asking)
- One npm package at the repo root (Node 20 or newer, npm). Next.js App Router app with Tailwind v4 (design tokens above in the @theme block of app/globals.css). Shared code in lib/. MCP server in mcp/server.ts, run with `npx tsx mcp/server.ts` (stdio, @modelcontextprotocol/sdk). It imports from lib/, so there is no second package. The MCP server loads .env itself; MCP client configs never contain secrets and are never committed.
- Database: Postgres via the `postgres` npm library. With DATABASE_URL set (Supabase pooler, the Vercel copy) it uses that. Without it, the app runs its own built-in Postgres (lib/local-db.ts, official binaries from `embedded-postgres`) with the data in `.data/` (PRACTICE_OS_DATA_DIR), listening only on 127.0.0.1 with a generated password; it generates CONNECTIONS_ENCRYPTION_KEY there and migrates itself on start, backing up first (lib/local-backup.ts). Supabase is never migrated automatically. `npm test` uses a throwaway built-in database unless DATABASE_URL is exported in the shell. All tables in schema practice_os. Never read or change any other schema. RLS enabled on every table with no policies. Never use the anon key, and never put secrets in NEXT_PUBLIC_ variables. Migrations are numbered SQL files in db/migrations, run by `npm run db:migrate`. `npm run db:reset` truncates only the demo-data tables and reseeds the fake practice; it never touches settings or stored connections (her Muse key). Any fresh-install test uses its own separate Supabase project, never this DATABASE_URL.
- Meta API: lib/meta.ts, base URL https://api.meta.ai/v1, key in MODEL_API_KEY. Chat Completions for muse-spark-1.3 and its tool calls. Responses API for muse-image-1.0, used only by `npm run smoke` for the demo headshot (follow github.com/meta-models/meta-model-cookbook folder 05_muse_image). POST /v1/asr/transcribe for muse-voice-transcribe-1.0. Copy request shapes from the cookbook; never guess. Never use a model ID ending in "-contributor". Every call writes a row to practice_os.model_calls (model, feature, tokens, ms, ok).
- Aeon's memory: Aeon is a persistent agent, not a stateless chatbot. `aeon/SOUL.md` (committed) holds who Aeon is, its voice and its hard rules. MEMORY.md holds what Aeon has learned about this practice: preferences, decisions, facts and what worked. Memories live in practice_os.memories so the local app and the Vercel copy share them. They are rendered to `aeon/MEMORY.md` (gitignored) and exposed as an MCP resource, so any agent can read the same memory. SOUL.md and the relevant memories go into every Aeon model call. Aeon saves memories through a `remember` tool, with each one audited, and a reflection pass (lib/aeon/reflect.ts: after every 10th message, when a chat goes quiet, and once a day when the app is opened) rereads conversations and adds, updates or retires memories; Muse only proposes, plain code checks each change (her own words as evidence, practice topics only, no critical numbers) before it saves. The clinician can view, edit and delete them on a Memory page. Until there is a BAA, memories hold practice-business facts only: no client names, no client details, no clinical content. A plain-code filter refuses those.
- Approvals: agents and tools only INSERT into approvals (kind, title, preview, payload, status='pending'). POST /api/approvals/[id]/approve claims the row atomically (UPDATE ... WHERE status='pending' RETURNING) and runs lib/executors/<kind>.ts. Status ends as done or failed with result or error. Executors are the only code that sends, posts or books. DRY_RUN=true makes executors log instead of act. Her approval mode (Manual, Auto, Bypass) decides which kinds run without her tap; anything for a client always waits. One exception skips approvals: when the crisis check fires, lib/safety.ts shows the fixed crisis reply and writes the alert to audit_log, and Admin shows it as an urgent item at the top. Nothing is texted.
- Connections (Settings › Connections, lib/connect): her own Muse key is the one connection. She pastes it in onboarding or Connections; it is checked and kept encrypted. The key route answers only on localhost, never on the Vercel copy.
- Stored sign-ins (practice_os.connections) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in lib/secrets.ts using CONNECTIONS_ENCRYPTION_KEY (in .env and Vercel only). Only lib/connections.ts reads or writes that table; the database refuses plain tokens.
- Tests: vitest, `npm test`. The same app also deploys to Vercel as a view-only demo; both copies share the database.
- Vercel copy: every page and API route sits behind DEMO_PASSWORD (view only), except the daily memory cron, which needs CRON_SECRET. Approve buttons there also need ADMIN_PASSWORD, which only I have.

- Never charge or pay per referral or per client. No fees, gifts or anything of value tied to referrals.
- Secrets live in .env and in Vercel settings only. .env, .mcp.json, .DS_Store and test/ media are in .gitignore and never committed. A gitleaks scan runs before every commit.
